# Pano 360 – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Pano 360 is designed to work entirely on your iPhone. **We do not collect, store, or
share any personal data**, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What the app accesses and why

| Permission | Purpose | Leaves your device? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Taking the photos that are stitched into your panorama. | No |
| Motion & orientation (ARKit, CoreMotion) | Guiding the capture, aligning photos, and the "look around" mode of the viewer. | No |
| Location (optional) | Embedding GPS position and compass heading in your panoramas so they appear on the map. | Only inside panoramas you choose to share |
| Photos (optional) | Saving finished panoramas to your photo library / the "Pano 360" album. | No |

## Your data

- Photos, panoramas and capture data are stored only in the app's private storage on
  your device and in your photo library if you choose to save them.
- Panoramas you share or export (including "Export capture data") are sent only where
  you choose to send them. They may contain the embedded location if location access is
  enabled. You can turn off location access for Pano 360 in iOS Settings at any time.
- Deleting a panorama in the app also deletes its stored capture data. Deleting the app
  removes all of its data.

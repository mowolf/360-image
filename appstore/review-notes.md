# Notes for App Review

Pano 360 captures 360° panoramas (photo spheres) and needs a **physical iPhone with ARKit**. The camera is
the first screen.

How to test (about 1 minute):
1. Launch the app and allow camera access. Location and Photos access are optional.
2. Read the short introduction card and tap **Get started**.
3. Point the white circle in the middle of the screen at the orange dot and hold still. A photo is taken
   automatically and the next dot appears. Take at least 2–3 photos by following the dots.
4. Tap **Done**. The panorama is stitched on the device (a few seconds to a minute) and opens in the 360° viewer.
5. Drag to look around. The share button offers "Share…" and "Save to Photos".
6. The grid button (bottom left on the camera screen) opens the gallery. The gear icon there opens Settings.

No login or account needed.

In-app purchase: the first 3 panoramas are free. After that the camera screen shows an unlock card
for "Pano 360 Unlimited" (non-consumable, `com.moritzwolf.pano360.unlimited`). "Restore purchases"
is in Settings (gallery → gear icon).

# Screenshots

App Store Connect requires iPhone screenshots for the **6.9" display** (1320 × 2868 or 1290 × 2796, portrait).
Smaller sizes are generated from those. Up to 10 are allowed. The app needs a real device (ARKit), so take
them on an iPhone 16/17 Pro Max, or on any iPhone, then scale or pad to 1320 × 2868.

Suggested set (5):
1. **Capture**: camera screen with the dot inside the circle and a few captured photos pinned around.
   Caption: "Follow the dots – photos are taken automatically"
2. **Coverage**: pointing up at the ceiling or sky with many photos already placed.
   Caption: "Capture the whole sphere"
3. **Stitching**: the progress screen. Caption: "Stitched on your iPhone in seconds"
4. **Viewer**: a beautiful panorama in the 360° viewer. Caption: "Look around in 360°"
5. **Gallery**: several panoramas. Caption: "All your spheres – ready to share"

Tip: take screenshots with side button + volume up. Add captions with Keynote / Figma or App Store
Connect's built-in frames.

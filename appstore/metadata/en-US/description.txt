Capture the whole world around you – up, down and all the way around – and turn it into a seamless 360° photo sphere, right on your iPhone.

Pano 360 brings back the simple, guided way of shooting photo spheres: point the circle at the dot, hold still, and the photo is taken automatically. A new dot then appears where the next photo belongs, and your captured photos are pinned into the camera view so you always see what's still missing.

HOW IT WORKS
• Aim the circle at the dot – photos are taken automatically when you hold still
• Follow the dots around the horizon, up to the sky and down to the floor
• Tap Done at any time – or capture the full sphere
• Pano 360 aligns, blends and exports a 360° panorama in seconds

PROFESSIONAL-GRADE STITCHING, ON DEVICE
• Precise alignment using ARKit motion tracking plus feature matching and bundle adjustment
• Parallax correction with depth from LiDAR, ARKit and on-device machine learning – nearby objects line up, even when hand-held
• Smart seams that route around people and objects instead of cutting through them
• Multi-band blending and exposure balancing for invisible transitions
• HDR capture keeps lamps, windows and skies from blowing out
• Automatic vignetting correction and a perfectly level horizon

SHARE IN 360°
• Exports standard equirectangular JPEGs with Photo Sphere (GPano) metadata
• Viewable as interactive 360° photos in Google Photos, Facebook and other 360° viewers
• Optional automatic saving to a "Pano 360" album in Photos
• GPS location and compass heading are embedded, so your spheres appear on the map

EXPLORE
• Built-in 360° viewer: drag or move your phone to look around, pinch to zoom
• All processing happens on your iPhone – no account, no uploads, no tracking

Requires an iPhone with ARKit support. Works best on iPhones with LiDAR (Pro models), but every supported iPhone gets depth-based parallax correction.
